How to play

  • Take turns placing stones. Line up five in a row to win.
  • 12 bombs are hidden on the board. Place a stone on one and Nimbo drops it: your stone and every stone around it get blown away, and you lose your turn.
  • Usually the blast clears a 3×3 area. Sometimes it's a big one: 5×5.
  • On any move there's a 1 in 6 chance Spook the ghost shows up and challenges you to rock-paper-scissors. Lose and your turn is over. Win and your stone goes down with a shining shield, and bombs can't blow it away.
